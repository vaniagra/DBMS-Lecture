# Instructor Guide

## Session Objective

Students can define cloud-native vs distributed at an introductory level and connect replication to a future consistency problem. They must not “finish CAP” today.

## Course Outcome Mapping

```text
CO1  Cloud-native databases
CO4  Introduction to distributed databases
```

## Flow

```text
0–8 min     Two campuses, 20-minute outage
8–22 min    Cloud-native: managed, you still design
22–40 min   Fragments vs replicas
40–50 min   PBL 04 double-allocation if two sites
50–60 min   Exit; remind M1 presentations S08
```

## Do not

Teach 2PC in full. Name it as “Unit 4.”

## Exit Question

Why does replication of `blood_unit.status` need extra rules? Four sentences.
