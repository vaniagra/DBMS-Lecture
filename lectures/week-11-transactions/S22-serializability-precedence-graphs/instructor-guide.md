# Instructor Guide

## Session Objective

Every student draws at least two precedence graphs. One cyclic, one acyclic. They can define conflict and recoverable schedule at overview level.

## Course Outcome Mapping

```text
CO4  Concurrent executions, serializability, recoverability, graphs, conflict serializability
```

## Flow

```text
0–8 min     Why interleaving
8–20 min    Conflict definition
20–40 min   Live graph (cycle)
40–52 min   Students: second schedule
52–60 min   Recoverability one slide
```

## Materials

Print 4 schedules. No laptops required for the graph.

## Exit Question

When is there an edge T1 → T2 in the precedence graph?
