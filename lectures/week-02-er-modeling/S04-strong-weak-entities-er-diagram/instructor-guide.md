# Instructor Guide

## Session Objective

Students can identify weak vs strong sets, draw a correct identifying relationship, and produce a small ER diagram that a stranger can read.

## Course Outcome Mapping

```text
CO1  ER diagram, strong and weak entity sets
```

## 50–60 Minute Session Flow

```text
0–8 min     Hook: bed 12 in every ward
8–20 min    Definition + notation drill
20–35 min   Live draw: Ward–Bed–Patient–Admission
35–48 min   Students draw Exam Room–Seat–Student on paper
48–55 min   Gallery: missing double lines
55–60 min   Exit
```

## Board Plan

Draw Ward–Bed exactly as in the student reference. Leave it up through Milestone 2.

## Misconceptions

- Composite keys make an entity weak (no)
- Weak entities cannot have relationships to others (they can, carefully)

## PBL connection

Design review 24 Sep: you will ask “show me one weak entity or argue you have none.”

## Exit Question

Give the full key of a weak entity in four sentences using Ward and Bed.
