## Problem

Hostel: Hostel, Room (room_no unique per hostel), Resident, Complaint (numbered per hostel per year).

## Tasks

1. Classify each as strong or weak.
2. Draw ER with keys, partial keys, and one relationship attribute `filed_on`.
3. State participation of Room in “located_in” Hostel.

## Test

Complaint as strong with only `complaint_no` (1, 2, 3…) campus-wide is **wrong** unless you invent a surrogate and say so.
