# Plagiarism / originality review

Do **not** auto-fail on similarity.

Look for:

- Identical ER layouts and invented entity names
- Identical 20-query files including the same aliases
- Reports that never mention a constraint that the schema also lacks
- Git history: one commit “final” on due night with 4000 lines
- AI tone with no declaration

Then: viva. If they can rebuild the precedence graph and explain a join, similarity may be coincidence or allowed discussion.

If they cannot, grade the work as not theirs — that is the finding, not Turnitin’s number.
