# Problem — Campus resources

Track classrooms, laboratories, equipment, bookings, utilization, conflicts, maintenance, faculty use, student use.

**Key questions the database must answer**

- Which rooms are booked twice for the same slot?
- Which labs are under 20% utilized over 30 days?
- Which equipment is repeatedly down after “repair”?

Not a room-booking website. The product is **integrity + utilization queries**.
